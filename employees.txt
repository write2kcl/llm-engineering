ABOUT ACME CORPORATION

Acme Corporation is an online retail company founded in 2008 by Margaret Ellison and Daniel Okafor in a rented garage in Boulder, Colorado. It started by selling refurbished electronics and grew into a general online marketplace that sells electronics, home goods, books, fashion, sports gear, toys and groceries. Acme's headquarters is at 1200 Larimer Street, Denver, Colorado. The company has about 4,720 employees, fulfillment centers in Denver, Dallas, Columbus and Reno, and a customer support hub in Tampa, Florida. In fiscal year 2025 Acme reported revenue of 3.2 billion US dollars and 11 million active customers.

Acme's mission statement is: "Everything you need, delivered with care." The company's four core values are Customer First, Own the Outcome, Keep It Simple, and Build Together.

AWARDS AND RECOGNITION

Acme gives out company awards every year at the Annual All-Hands meeting in December. Winners receive a crystal trophy, a cash bonus and two extra days of paid time off. The Employee of the Year bonus is 10,000 US dollars; all other awards carry a 5,000 US dollar bonus.

Employee of the Year 2025: Diego Fernandes, Reno Fulfillment Center General Manager. He was recognized for reaching 99.6 percent on-time shipping during the 2025 holiday season and reducing workplace injuries at the Reno site by 40 percent.

Employee of the Year 2024: Oliver Grant, Senior Software Engineer. He was recognized for rewriting the checkout service, which raised conversion rate by 3.1 percent.

Employee of the Year 2023: Chloe Martin, Customer Support Team Lead. She was recognized for creating the "first contact resolution" playbook now used by all support teams.

Employee of the Year 2022: Megan Lowe, Category Manager, Home and Kitchen. She was recognized for launching the Acme Home brand, which reached 120 million US dollars in sales in its first year.

Innovation Award 2025: Ananya Reddy, Staff Engineer, for the semantic product search project, which increased search-to-purchase rate by 12 percent.

Rookie of the Year 2025: Arjun Mehta, Junior Data Analyst, for the returns analytics dashboard, which helped cut the return rate on electronics from 9 percent to 6.5 percent.

Customer Champion Award 2025: Aiko Tanaka, VP of Customer Experience, for raising company-wide CSAT from 88 percent to 93 percent.

Leadership Excellence Award 2025: Sofia Delgado, COO, for leading the Reno fulfillment center launch and the same-day delivery expansion to 12 cities.

Rising Star Award 2025: Lucy Bennett, Social Media Manager, for tripling Acme's social media following.

Founders' Award 2025 (lifetime contribution): Robert Kowalski, VP of Merchandising, for 11 years of building Acme's product catalog from 2,000 to over 3 million items.

Margaret Ellison was also named "Retail CEO of the Year 2024" by the (fictional) National E-Commerce Association.
